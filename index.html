Privacy Policy — In and Out Breathing Timer

Last updated: 2026-10-19

In and Out Breathing Timer works offline. We do not collect, receive, sell or share your app data. The app does not send data off your phone. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Data stored on your device

Your favorite presets, settings, language choice and session history are stored locally on your device. History includes session dates, durations and the inhale and exhale times used. This information is not uploaded to the developer or an app server.

Optional test sensor log

Sensor log (test) is off by default. If you enable it on Android, the app records accelerometer and gyroscope readings and breathing-timer events during a session. A CSV file is saved in the app's local files folder. It includes timestamps, selected timing and phone placement, app version, device manufacturer and model, and operating system details. The log is for testing motion sensors; it does not detect your breathing.

The app does not upload or share these files. Turning logging off stops future logging but does not delete existing files. If you manually copy a file to another device, you control that copy.

Android backup and device transfer

The current Android configuration allows operating-system backup. Depending on your device and system settings, Android may back up or transfer app data separately from the app. The developer does not receive or have access to these backups. Manage backups through your device's system and backup-provider settings.

Retention and deletion

Locally saved settings, history and test files remain until removed. You can clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app to remove its local data. Manage any system backups or manually copied CSV files separately; uninstalling does not erase those copies.

Audience and changes

This app is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not collect personal information from users of any age. We will update this policy if the app's data handling changes.
